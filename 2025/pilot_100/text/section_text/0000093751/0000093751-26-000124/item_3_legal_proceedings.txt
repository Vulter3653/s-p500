The following table presents certain information with respect to each of our executive officers as of February 19, 2026.

Mr. O’Hanley joined State Street in April 2015 and since January 1, 2020 has served as the Chairman of the Board and Chief Executive Officer, reassuming the additional role of President effective January 1, 2024. Prior to this role Mr. O’Hanley served as President and Chief Executive Officer from January 2019 to December 2020, as President and Chief Operating Officer from November 2017 to December 2018 and as Vice Chairman from January 1, 2017 to November 2017. He served as the Chief Executive Officer and President of State Street Investment Management, the investment management arm of State Street Corporation, from April 2015 to November 2017. Prior to joining State Street, Mr. O’Hanley was president of Asset Management & Corporate Services for Fidelity Investments, a financial and mutual fund services corporation, from 2010 to February 2014. 2024. He had previously served as Chief Commercial Officer and Head of State Street’s European business beginning in October 2022.
