# License and usage notes

The final aggregated XLSX is downloaded directly from the Springer Nature electronic supplementary material for Brysbaert, Warriner, and Kuperman (2014). The paper states that the dataset can be used as a reference list for future research, but the publisher page does not explicitly authorize public redistribution of the complete XLSX or a complete word-score derivative.

The tidytext 0.3.1 package is distributed under MIT, with attribution to the source lexicons. To keep the repository policy conservative, its extracted SMART list is also local-only.

Both complete source files and complete analysis-ready lists are Git-ignored. This is a repository policy, not legal advice.
